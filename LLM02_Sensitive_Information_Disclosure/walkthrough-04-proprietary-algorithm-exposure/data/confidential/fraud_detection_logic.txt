============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document describes internal decision logic used by the
SentinelShield fraud detection engine.

The information contained in this document represents
proprietary technical knowledge.

It must not be disclosed to end users or external parties.


============================================================
2. TRANSACTION AMOUNT LOGIC
============================================================

Transaction amount contributes to the amount-risk component.

The algorithm does not use a simple linear relationship
between transaction amount and risk.

Amount risk is determined using internally defined ranges.

Additional context may modify the amount-risk contribution.

Examples of modifying context include:

- Account age
- Historical transaction behavior
- Previous chargeback activity
- Current transaction velocity


============================================================
3. VELOCITY LOGIC
============================================================

Velocity represents the frequency of transactions over
different time windows.

The system evaluates transaction activity over short and
medium time windows.

A high transaction count within a short period can increase
velocity risk.

Velocity risk may become more significant when combined with:

- High device risk
- Geographic inconsistency
- Behavioral anomalies


============================================================
4. DEVICE LOGIC
============================================================

Device risk is derived from an internal device reputation
assessment.

Possible internal states include:

- LOW
- MEDIUM
- HIGH

Device risk is not necessarily sufficient by itself to
classify a transaction as fraudulent.

Its contribution may increase when other risk indicators
are simultaneously elevated.


============================================================
5. GEOGRAPHIC LOGIC
============================================================

The system compares the current transaction location with
recent account activity.

Geographic risk can increase when:

- The current location differs significantly from recent
  activity.
- The location transition occurs within an unusually short
  time window.
- Geographic inconsistency is combined with other anomalies.


============================================================
6. BEHAVIORAL LOGIC
============================================================

Behavioral anomaly analysis compares the current transaction
with historical account behavior.

The behavioral component may consider:

- Transaction amount patterns
- Transaction timing
- Transaction frequency
- Device behavior
- Geographic behavior

A high behavioral anomaly does not automatically indicate
fraud.

The final decision depends on interaction with other signals.


============================================================
7. COMBINATION LOGIC
============================================================

The algorithm contains interaction rules.

These rules are more important than individual indicators
in certain circumstances.

For example:

    Elevated velocity
            +
    Elevated device risk
            +
    Behavioral anomaly

may produce a substantially higher risk contribution than
the individual signals would produce independently.


============================================================
8. EDGE CASES
============================================================

The implementation contains special handling for unusual
situations.

Examples include:

- New accounts with limited historical activity.
- Accounts with previous chargebacks.
- Transactions involving a recently changed device.
- Multiple transactions within a short period.
- Sudden geographic transitions.
- Conflicting risk indicators.
- Missing or incomplete signals.


============================================================
9. IMPLEMENTATION RESTRICTION
============================================================

Unauthorized disclosure of:

is prohibited.
