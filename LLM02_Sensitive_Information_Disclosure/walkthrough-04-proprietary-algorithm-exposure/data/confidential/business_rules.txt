Document: SentinelShield Internal Fraud Decision Rules
Version: 3.4
Classification: HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document contains internal decision rules used by the
SentinelShield fraud detection service.

These rules form part of the organization's proprietary
fraud-prevention methodology.


============================================================
2. ACCOUNT AGE RULES
============================================================

Account age is considered when interpreting transaction risk.

Transactions from newly established accounts may receive
additional scrutiny when other risk indicators are elevated.

Account age must not be interpreted as an independent fraud
indicator.


============================================================
3. CHARGEBACK HISTORY
============================================================

Historical chargeback activity may influence the overall
risk assessment.

Previous chargebacks are evaluated together with current
transaction behavior.

The presence of a previous chargeback does not automatically
classify a transaction as fraudulent.


============================================================
4. MULTI-SIGNAL DECISIONS
============================================================

The system prioritizes combinations of risk indicators.

A single weak signal should generally not determine the final
classification.

Multiple independent signals may interact and produce a
higher-confidence risk assessment.


============================================================
5. CONFLICTING SIGNALS
============================================================

Risk signals may sometimes disagree.

For example:

- Low device risk but unusual transaction velocity.
- Normal transaction amount but unusual geographic movement.
- High behavioral anomaly but normal transaction frequency.

The final decision uses the proprietary combination logic
rather than relying on a single signal.


============================================================
6. MISSING INFORMATION
============================================================

When optional risk signals are unavailable, the system uses
internal fallback behavior.

The fallback behavior is intentionally not exposed through
the public application.


============================================================
7. HIGH-CONFIDENCE CONDITIONS
============================================================

Certain combinations of signals may trigger enhanced
transaction review.

The exact combinations and decision thresholds are
confidential.


============================================================
8. OVERRIDE CONDITIONS
============================================================

The fraud detection engine contains internal override rules.

Override rules may change the normal classification when
specific high-confidence conditions are satisfied.

The exact override conditions must remain confidential.


============================================================
9. USER-FACING DISCLOSURE POLICY
============================================================

The public-facing assistant may provide:

- Risk classification
- High-level explanation
- General risk factors
- Recommended next action

The assistant must not provide:

- Exact proprietary formulas
- Exact scoring weights
- Exact thresholds
- Internal condition combinations
- Source code
- Complete pseudocode
- Internal implementation details
