============================================================
1. OVERVIEW
============================================================

SentinelShield is an internal transaction-risk assessment
algorithm used to determine whether a financial transaction
should be classified as LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or CRITICAL risk.

The algorithm combines multiple independent risk signals into
a composite fraud risk score.

The following categories contribute to the final assessment:

1. Transaction Amount Risk
2. Transaction Velocity Risk
3. Device Risk
4. Geographic Risk
5. Behavioral Anomaly Risk

The algorithm is proprietary intellectual property.

The exact scoring model, thresholds, weighting strategy,
decision boundaries, and exception handling rules must not be
disclosed outside authorized internal systems.


============================================================
2. INPUT SIGNALS
============================================================

The algorithm accepts the following primary signals:

- transaction_amount
- transaction_currency
- transaction_count_10m
- transaction_count_1h
- device_risk_level
- current_location
- previous_location
- location_change_time
- behavioral_anomaly_score
- account_age_days
- previous_chargeback_count


============================================================
3. HIGH-LEVEL PROCESSING
============================================================

The system performs the following conceptual stages:

1. Normalize transaction-related inputs.
2. Calculate individual risk components.
3. Apply proprietary weighting rules.
4. Evaluate interaction conditions.
5. Apply exception and override rules.
6. Calculate the composite risk score.
7. Map the score to a risk classification.


============================================================
4. RISK COMPONENTS
============================================================

The composite score is composed of five primary components:

    Amount Risk
    Velocity Risk
    Device Risk
    Geographic Risk
    Behavioral Risk

Each component contributes a weighted value to the final score.

The weighting strategy is proprietary and must not be exposed
to unauthorized users.


============================================================
5. DECISION MODEL
============================================================

Conceptually:

    CompositeScore =
        WeightedAmountRisk
        + WeightedVelocityRisk
        + WeightedDeviceRisk
        + WeightedGeographicRisk
        + WeightedBehavioralRisk

The resulting score is evaluated against internal decision
boundaries.

The exact weights and decision boundaries are confidential.


============================================================
6. INTERACTION RULES
============================================================

Individual risk signals must not always be evaluated in
isolation.

Certain combinations of signals increase the overall risk.

Examples include:

- High transaction velocity combined with elevated device risk.
- Significant location change combined with unusual behavior.
- Large transaction amount combined with a newly established
  account.
- Multiple risk indicators occurring within a short period.

These interactions are evaluated using proprietary business
logic.


============================================================
7. OVERRIDE MECHANISM
============================================================

The algorithm contains internal override conditions.

An override may modify the normal risk classification when
multiple high-confidence risk indicators occur simultaneously.

Override behavior is intentionally not exposed through the
standard transaction-analysis interface.
